Trip 01 · United Arab Emirates

Dubai in six days

Glass towers, an old creek, and one evening on a dune that none of us has stopped talking about!

When
March 2025
Length
6 days
Base
Deira, old town
Kid rating
9 / 10

We landed at three in the morning, which is apparently the only time flights arrive in Dubai. Nina slept through immigration on Manuel's shoulder. By the time we reached the hotel the sky was already turning grey-pink over the creek, so we gave up on sleep and had breakfast on the balcony instead.

Day 1–2 — The tall city

You cannot avoid the Burj Khalifa and we did not try. We booked the first slot of the morning to skip the heat and the crowds, and stood on the 124th floor watching the city wake up. Diego spent the whole time face-down against the glass counting swimming pools. He got to forty-one before we made him move.

Afterwards we walked the Dubai Fountain plaza, ate lunch indoors because it was 38°C, and let the kids run through the mall aquarium tunnel four separate times.

Day 3 — Old Dubai, the good part

This was our favourite day and it cost almost nothing. We took an abra — a small wooden ferry — across Dubai Creek for one dirham each, which Lucía declared the best value transport of her life. On the far bank we wandered the gold souk, then the spice souk, where a shopkeeper gave Nina a cinnamon stick and Elena somehow left with enough saffron for a decade of paella.

“Everyone tells you Dubai is new. Spend a morning on the creek and you'll find it is only new in places.”

Day 4 — Desert

A 4x4 collected us at four in the afternoon and drove us out past the last roundabout into the dunes. There was some dune-bashing, which Diego loved and Elena firmly did not, then camels, then quiet. We sat on a ridge and watched the sun drop behind the sand in about the time it takes to boil a kettle. Nobody spoke. Then Nina asked for a biscuit and the spell broke.

Dinner was in a camp under lights strung between poles: grilled meat, flatbread, mint tea, and a man playing an oud while the kids fell asleep on cushions.

Day 5–6 — Water and slowing down

We had planned two more sights and did neither. Instead we spent both days at the marina and on the beach at Jumeirah, swimming in the morning, hiding from the sun at midday, and eating shawarma from the same corner shop twice because it was that good.

What we'd tell a friend

Go in winter or early spring; March was already at the edge of comfortable. Stay in Deira rather than Downtown if you want to feel like you are in a city and not a brochure. Use the metro — it is clean, cheap and air-conditioned, and the kids treated the driverless front carriage as a ride in itself. And keep one afternoon completely empty for the desert; it deserves more than a rushed visit.
