We are the Rodríguez family. Every holiday we fill a memory card with pictures and a notebook with small stories — the good, the sweaty and the slightly lost. This is where we keep them.

About us

Two parents, three opinions on where to eat, and one shared love of an early flight.

  • Manuel Dad · route planner

    Books the flights, carries the camera, insists we leave for the airport three hours early.

  • Elena Mum · chief negotiator

    Finds the family-run restaurant everyone else walked past. Keeps the passports.

  • Lucía Age 13 · photographer

    Half the pictures on this site are hers. Refuses to photograph anything before 9am.

  • Diego Age 9 · snack officer

    Rates every trip by the quality of its breakfast buffet. Malaysia currently leads.

  • Nina Age 4 · morale

    Sleeps through take-off, wakes up for dessert, makes friends in every queue.

How we travel

We keep it simple: one bag each, one plan a day, and nothing booked for the afternoons. The best parts of both of these trips were the parts we did not plan — a rooftop with a view of a thunderstorm, a night market where nobody spoke our language and it did not matter at all.

“The photos are the souvenir. The stories are the reason we keep going back.”
